# 宣传片配乐提示词（给 Gemini 的音乐模型用）

视频是 76 秒、120 BPM、4/4 拍，一拍 0.5 秒，一小节 2 秒，第一个强拍就在 0 秒。画面上有几个地方必须踩上音乐：4.0 秒图标落定，是全片最重的一下；6 秒起进入稳定的律动；20 秒服务器启动，稍微提一下；32 秒起能量开始往上走，40 到 44 秒是蓄力；44 秒是第二个落点，从这里到 62 秒是全片最热闹的一段；62.5、63.0、63.5 秒依次落下「开服 / 排错 / 联机」三个词，最好有三下点状的重音；64 秒进入片尾，之后画面基本不动，音乐在 76 秒前渐弱到无声。

下面四版风格不同，挑一版先试。提示词用英文写，音乐模型对英文的理解一般更稳。模型不一定能精确对上每个秒数，生成出来以后拖进剪辑软件，把第一个强拍对齐到 0 秒，再看 4 秒和 44 秒那两下有没有落在画面上，差一点可以整体挪几帧。如果一次生成不够 76 秒，可以分两段生成（0–44 秒、44–76 秒），用同一版提示词、注明同一个调和速度，在 44 秒的落点处接上。

片子是给国内社交平台用的，配乐只要纯音乐，不要人声，也不要让模型模仿任何现成的游戏原声或歌手。

## 第一版：像素电子（最贴画面）

画面是方块拼图标、像素擦除转场，这版用芯片音色呼应它，又加上现代的鼓，不会显得老旧。

```
Instrumental chiptune-pop track for a 76-second software promo video, 120 BPM, 4/4, major key, bright and friendly.
Square-wave and pulse lead melodies layered with a modern punchy drum kit, warm sub bass, and soft synth pads.
Structure (timestamps in seconds):
0–4: quiet intro, a gentle arpeggio that slowly rises, ending in a short riser.
4.0: the strongest hit of the track — a big downbeat with a sparkling 8-bit burst.
6–20: steady, light groove with a catchy pulse-wave hook, clean and not busy.
20: a small lift (add claps and a second melody layer).
32–40: energy gradually rises, add hi-hats and a counter-melody.
40–44: build-up with a snare roll and riser.
44: second drop, the most energetic part, full drums and hook, until about 62.
62.5, 63.0, 63.5: three short accented hits.
64–76: outro, the melody slows down and fades out to silence by 76 seconds.
No vocals. Original melody only, do not imitate any existing game soundtrack.
```

## 第二版：轻快的 Lo-fi（适合 B 站、小红书的教程向）

画面底色是暖色的沙纸，这版用温暖的电钢琴和松弛的鼓，听着像「下班后和朋友开个服」。

```
Instrumental warm lo-fi hip-hop track for a 76-second app promo, 120 BPM (half-time feel), 4/4, cozy and optimistic.
Rhodes electric piano chords, soft plucked guitar, mellow bass, light vinyl texture, crisp but relaxed drums.
Structure (timestamps in seconds):
0–4: soft piano intro with a gentle swell.
4.0: a clear downbeat where drums and bass enter together.
6–20: laid-back groove with a simple, memorable piano motif.
20: small lift, a bright bell or glockenspiel layer joins.
32–44: slowly build energy, fuller chords, a tape-stop or riser at 44.
44–62: the fullest section, livelier drums and a counter-melody.
62.5, 63.0, 63.5: three gentle accented chord stabs.
64–76: outro, strip back to piano and fade to silence by 76 seconds.
No vocals, no heavy distortion.
```

## 第三版：有冲劲的电子流行（适合抖音、快手的开头抓人）

节奏感最强，两个落点都做得很满，适合要在前几秒抓住人的平台。

```
Instrumental upbeat electronic pop track for a 76-second tech promo, 120 BPM, 4/4, energetic, positive, modern.
Bright synth plucks, sidechained pads, punchy kick and claps, tight bass, airy risers.
Structure (timestamps in seconds):
0–4: minimal filtered intro building tension, with a quick riser.
4.0: a strong drop that hits exactly on the downbeat.
6–20: driving but clean groove with a pluck hook, room for on-screen text.
20: a small lift with an extra synth layer.
32–40: build energy step by step.
40–44: big build-up, snare roll, white-noise riser, a short silence right before 44.
44–62: the main drop, the most energetic part of the track.
62.5, 63.0, 63.5: three punchy staccato hits.
64–76: outro, filter the track down and fade out completely by 76 seconds.
No vocals, no vocal chops.
```

## 第四版：明亮的原声小编制（最不吵）

马林巴、拨弦、尤克里里这类乐器，干净、亲切，适合画面信息多、想让人看清字的时候。

```
Instrumental bright acoustic track for a 76-second friendly software explainer, 120 BPM, 4/4, cheerful and light.
Marimba, pizzicato strings, ukulele, glockenspiel, soft hand percussion and a light kick drum.
Structure (timestamps in seconds):
0–4: a single marimba line, quiet and curious.
4.0: the whole ensemble enters on a bright downbeat.
6–20: playful bouncy groove, uncluttered.
20: small lift, add glockenspiel sparkle.
32–44: gradually fuller, strings join, a short crescendo into 44.
44–62: the fullest and most joyful section.
62.5, 63.0, 63.5: three plucked accents.
64–76: outro, back to marimba alone, ritardando feel, fade to silence by 76 seconds.
No vocals.
```

## 用的时候注意

画面在 64 秒以后基本不动，只有片尾卡片。配乐如果收得早，可以把视频也剪到 70 秒左右，不影响内容。平台自动取第一帧当封面时会是一张空的沙纸底，发的时候手动选 4.9 秒左右（图标和字标都在）那一帧当封面。

## 选定的一版

作者 11-06 选了生成出来的 Warmth by the Window，文件在 `music/` 里。它的速度是 124 BPM，前奏也比画面长，不用再拖进剪辑软件对齐：`music.js` 会把它整首放慢约 4.9%、裁掉开头，让两个重拍落在 4 秒和 44 秒，再合进两个视频。同一批生成的另外三首（Ready for Launch、The Gentle Launch、Brightest Morning）没有放进仓库。
